Privacy Policy

GameRoster uses Google APIs only to create and update spreadsheets that the app itself creates in the authorizing user's Google Drive (scope: drive.file). The app cannot access any other files.

Google OAuth tokens are stored on the app's server and are used solely to export event reports. Data is not shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
